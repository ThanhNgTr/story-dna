## Synopsis

Linh Khuê is dumped, broke, and numb. A flash-forward cold open spoils the premise: she will end
up sheltering a dragon prince. Pressed into a favor by her friend Hân Nghi, she finds not a human
nephew but Cảnh Viêm, a horned, golden-eyed boy abandoned outside an elite academy. Silence and
hot cocoa thaw him; a shared dinner, a running joke about his "royal horns", and stolen fruit
build a found-family bond, cracked only by a cruel voice message from her ex, Thế Khang, which
Viêm defends her against. That night Viêm secretly summons his idolized older brother for a staged
"rescue", wanting him to see Linh Khuê's worth. The brother, Cảnh Uy, descends in a meteor-like
blaze of golden light; his eyes flare with the ancient, involuntary fated-mate signal the instant
he sees her, which he denies. Comedy (he secretly devours her cooking despite refusing it) gives
way to escalating melodrama: a morning-after double-identity bombshell reveals Viêm is heir to the
ancient Hắc Kim dragon bloodline and Cảnh Uy is the feared Dragon Emperor of two realms. Public
humiliation from Thế Khang at a cafe pushes Viêm's temper into a dragon-rage eruption; windows
explode; Cảnh Uy strides through the wreckage, forces Khang to his knees, publicly marks Linh Khuê
with a glowing dragon seal, and turns on Khang with an absolute, verdict-like threat — cut off as
the reference ends.

## Important: the recap device inflates the runtime

The reference re-tells its own first act in the middle (t=1423.93-1918.93) and then again in act 3,
with VO lines repeated near-verbatim. That produces duplicate-looking beats: **two** meteor
arrivals, **two** aura ignitions, **two** cafe humiliations. This is deliberate (viewers jump in
mid-series), not a story error.

Consequence for the Story Template: the unique story spine is roughly the first half of the
reference plus act 3's escalated climax. The template in SKILL.md is built on the **deduplicated**
spine and treats the recap as a separate storytelling device, not a beat.

## World

- **Setting**: a modern city where dragons rule the sky and ordinary humans scrape by on the
  ground, with a hidden dragon aristocracy living alongside human society.
- **Rules**: involuntary unfakeable golden aura on meeting a fated mate (765.68); dragon-clan
  children born with horns, tail, slit pupils (186.07); a dragon lord's wrath can force a room to
  kneel and leaves glowing sigils on skin (1383.88); meteor-like arrivals of golden light with a
  thunderous impact (583.46); communicator-watch tech (489.18).
- **Locations**: Học Viện Tinh Hỏa academy (gothic gate, supercars, chauffeurs); Linh Khuê's
  apartment (worn, tape-patched window, warm amber lamps, Hello Kitty apron); the mansion
  "dinh thự" (cold, lonely, own French chef); upscale glass-walled cafe (repeated humiliation and
  the climactic rescue).
- **Motifs**: cold wind/rain; gold light overhead as hierarchy and fate; worn vs. glamorous
  clothing; phone/voice messages as judgment vectors; hot food/drink as care and trust; horns,
  tail and eyes glinting as emotion tells; the body betraying stated intentions; broken glass as
  power's aftermath; the apartment key and taped window as class-contrast props.
