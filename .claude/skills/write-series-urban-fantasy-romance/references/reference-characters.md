## char_01 — Linh Khuê (main, protagonist/narrator)

- **Archetype**: the overlooked caretaker who turns out to matter most.
- **Plot function**: emotional POV and engine of both the found-family and romance plots; her
  kindness toward the boy sets every later reveal in motion.
- **Want**: to survive the fallout of her breakup, keep her dignity, and get by.
- **Need**: to believe she deserves to be seen and valued without earning it through status.
- **Flaw**: internalized belief that she is worthless/beneath notice, conditioned by her ex and
  her poverty.
- **Secret**: none.
- **Visual signature**: woman mid-20s, wavy ash/dirty-blonde hair in a loose braid, bun or updo,
  fair complexion, light eyes; worn taupe/beige coat and chunky scarf outdoors, pink/blush knit
  sweater and Hello Kitty apron at home; modest apartment with a tape-patched window.
- **Voice**: dense, literary first-person retrospective voiceover carrying most of the
  storytelling, interleaved with soft, hesitant or gently teasing spoken dialogue.
- **Sample lines**:
  - "Ngay từ đầu tôi đã biết thằng bé không thuộc về mình."
  - "Tôi là Linh Khuê. Ảnh của anh gửi mờ quá, nên tôi tưởng cảnh viêm là cháu của ai đó."
  - "Đó là một sự hiểu lầm. Tôi xin lỗi."
  - "Khoảng cách giữa chúng tôi không chỉ là tiền bạc, còn là khoảng cách giữa mặt đất dưới chân tôi và bầu trời trên những tầng mây."

## char_02 — Cảnh Viêm (main, child catalyst)

- **Archetype**: the precocious child catalyst who forces the adults together.
- **Plot function**: bridges the protagonist and the male lead; his secret scheming literally
  causes the brother's arrival and the romance plot.
- **Want**: to stay with her instead of returning to the cold mansion; to protect her.
- **Need**: to be trusted and seen rather than judged, after being abandoned at the academy gate.
- **Flaw**: guarded hostility that pushes away the people who could care for him.
- **Secret**: he engineered his brother's visit from the start to prove her worth.
- **Visual signature**: boy about 7-10, curly golden-blond hair, small gold horns/spike ornaments,
  golden/amber vertical-slit-pupil eyes that glow with emotion and narrow to slits with scale-like
  skin when enraged, reptilian tail, ornate black-and-gold military-style jacket with crest.
- **Voice**: sharp and defensive at first, softening into sulky affection, precocious and proud
  when discussing his brother, feral when enraged.
- **Sample lines**:
  - "Không đời nào!"
  - "Sao cô cứ luôn hành động như thể mình không đáng để người khác nhìn thấy vậy?"
  - "Cô cho tôi ăn. Cô chưa bao giờ cố lợi dụng tôi. Cô không có gì phải xấu hổ cả. Nên đừng cố lẩn trốn nữa."
  - "Ngươi chán sống rồi à?"

## char_03 — Cảnh Uy (main, male lead)

- **Archetype**: the cold, all-powerful lord whose facade cracks only for the heroine.
- **Plot function**: antagonist-turned-protector; his power resolves every physical threat and his
  denial/reveal of the fated-mate bond drives the romance.
- **Want**: to retrieve his brother and maintain his aloof, controlled image.
- **Need**: to allow himself to be seen wanting something ordinary — a home, a meal, a woman who
  doesn't want anything from him.
- **Flaw**: suppresses and denies his own feelings, hiding behind rank and control.
- **Secret**: he is the Dragon Emperor of two realms, and his body gives an involuntary,
  unfakeable fated-mate reaction to her.
- **Visual signature**: tall man, long wavy golden hair, golden/amber eyes with vertical slit
  pupils that glow orange with power, ornate black baroque/cloaked jacket with gold dragon
  embroidery and chains; arrives via a meteor-like lightning descent.
- **Voice**: cold, clipped, formal, self-assured; narration calls him "lạnh lùng, cao ngạo"; drops
  to a low, absolute, verdict-like register when enraged.
- **Sample lines**:
  - "Cô cho thằng bé ăn thứ này sao? Chẳng có chút giá trị dinh dưỡng nào. Tôi không rảnh để ăn nữa đâu."
  - "Ở thành phố này, nếu tôi nói chúng ta cùng một thế giới thì chính là như vậy."
  - "Nếu có chuyện gì xảy ra, tôi sẽ có mặt."
  - "Dám công khai cô ấy sao?"

## char_04 — Thế Khang (supporting, antagonist)

- **Archetype**: the classist ex who needs to put the heroine down to feel superior.
- **Plot function**: generates the public-humiliation beats that twice force the male lead's power
  to intervene, and triggers the climactic eruption.
- **Want**: to flaunt his new relationship and reassert superiority over her.
- **Need**: none shown — played as a static antagonist.
- **Flaw**: cruelty masking insecurity about status.
- **Visual signature**: man mid/late 20s, dark wavy hair, clean-shaven, tailored dark three-piece
  suit; seen briefly in person, otherwise a phone voice / caller ID; a "Derik" voice-message header
  appears once alongside his name (unresolved discrepancy).
- **Voice**: curt and dismissive in person; smug, cutting, condescending by voice message and in
  public; collapses into terrified silence when overpowered.
- **Sample lines**:
  - "Chà chà Linh Khuê, tôi đã bảo cô rồi mà. Cô thực sự chạm đến đáy mới rồi đấy. Giờ lại xách mông đi giữ trẻ cho mấy đứa nhà giàu à?"
  - "Cô không sinh ra cho cuộc sống đó, chưa bao giờ."
  - "Thằng con hoang lai tạp của con bảo mẫu mạt hạng nghĩ mình có quyền nói chuyện với tao thế à?"

## char_05 — Hân Nghi (supporting, ally)

- **Archetype**: the well-meaning friend whose favor sets the plot in motion and who delivers the
  big reveals.
- **Plot function**: inciting-incident device and exposition conduit for both identity reveals.
- **Want**: to offload her husband's dragon-clan relative while she travels, then to warn and
  protect her friend.
- **Flaw**: her loyalty appears compromised at the cafe (likely continuity inconsistency).
- **Visual signature**: woman mid-20s to 30s, dark brown wavy or bobbed hair, camel/beige coat or
  elegant dress, small gold earrings, sometimes a rolling suitcase at the airport.
- **Voice**: urgent, apologetic, rushed on the phone; sharp and commanding when confronting the
  antagonist; sneering in the inconsistent cafe appearance.
- **Sample lines**:
  - "Linh Khuê! Trời đất ơi! Mình vừa nghe toàn bộ chuyện từ chồng mình. Cậu đón nhầm người rồi."
  - "Đó là Cảnh Viêm, đứa trẻ quý giá nhất của dòng máu Hắc Kim cổ đại và anh trai thằng bé."
  - "Thế Khang, ngậm cái miệng thối của anh lại."

1. Introduce the protagonist through first-person VO narrating her own humiliation before any
   other character speaks. *Ref: t=6.59-26.7, cold open.*
2. Delay a major character's name until several scenes after their first appearance; let the
   visual signature and relationships carry identification. *Ref: Viêm seen 186.07, named 419.75;
   Uy seen 598.63, named 2253.33.*
3. Reveal a cold character's hidden warmth through an involuntary physical tell, never dialogue.
   *Ref: ear-flush 977.73 and 2404.55; secretly eating refused food 890.21 and 2314.15.*
4. Give the child a dual function — comic relief and emotional truth-teller who says what the
   adults won't. *Ref: t=2071.32, "Sao cô cứ luôn hành động như thể mình không đáng để người khác
